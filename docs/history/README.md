# History

These documents recorded earlier states of Meridia and are kept unchanged in substance so
that the current freeze can be checked against the sequence that produced it. They are not
current. Where a statement here conflicts with the README, `docs/DESIGN.md`,
`docs/SUBMISSION_FORMAT.md`, or the frozen bar receipt under
`bars/national-v14-standard/`, the current file wins.

- `V4_DECISIONS.md` is the dated working log of the version-four passes, from the first
  generator decisions through the seventh freeze attempt. It is the only record of why each
  bar, rate, and profile was chosen. Its commands are written against the environment
  variables defined at the head of the file rather than against any one machine's paths.
- `V4_OBLIGATIONS.md` answers four proof obligations against a tree in which no bar was
  frozen, which the shipping `bars/national-v14-standard` has since superseded. The world
  and evidence trees its readings were taken on are not committed.
- `RELEASE_CONTRACT_V0.md` is the version-three participant contract, with a six-column
  release, a detailed table, and a county allocation. The version-four contract in
  `docs/SUBMISSION_FORMAT.md` replaced it and the two are not compatible.
- `RUBRIC_MAP.md` and `PRE_SUBMISSION_AUDIT.md` map the work to an external review process.
  They belong with the packaging repository rather than here and are held under history
  until they land there.
- `FRONTIER_TRIALS.md` registers a trial protocol for that same external process. The one
  sentence in it that binds the science, that a bar is never tightened to manufacture
  failure, is carried in `docs/DESIGN.md` under "How a bar is set".
